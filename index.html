Welcome to Mock & Roll

You're standing on the shooting floor, looking into the LED volume.

  1. 1Drag to look around; WASD to walk. Plan shows it from above.
  2. 2Open Add or Stage on the left to bring in decks, sets and props, then drag them into place.
  3. 3Open Camera to pick a lens, open the Viewfinder, and save or print the shot.
